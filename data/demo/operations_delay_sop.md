# SentinelAI Demo Company — Shipment Delay SOP

**Document type:** Synthetic demonstration procedure  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose
Guide the operations team in reviewing shipment delays and choosing appropriate next steps.

## 2. When to Escalate
Escalate a shipment when:
- The carrier reports a delay or exception.
- The estimated arrival time has passed without confirmed delivery.
- A critical shipment may miss its required delivery window.
- Available information is insufficient to assess the disruption reliably.

## 3. Required Investigation
Before recommending an action, verify:
- Latest shipment status and timestamp.
- Estimated arrival and any revised delivery estimate.
- Origin, destination, carrier and shipping mode.
- Available weather, route and relevant disruption-news evidence.
- Product urgency, supplier constraints and inventory impact, when available.

Distinguish confirmed facts from possible contributors. Weather, news reports or route alternatives alone do not prove the cause of a specific delay.

## 4. Alternative Routes
Consider alternative routes only when the route is relevant to the shipment's transport mode. Compare estimated travel time, distance, known restrictions and operational feasibility. A calculated route is not confirmation of carrier availability or a guaranteed delivery time.

## 5. Alternative Suppliers
Consider only suppliers with verified product compatibility and appropriate approval. Confirm stock or capacity, lead time, delivery coverage and relevant quality requirements before recommending substitution. If these details are unavailable, report that supplier feasibility cannot yet be verified.

## 6. Decision and Communication
The operations team must approve rerouting, carrier changes, supplier substitutions and revised customer commitments. Record the evidence reviewed, unresolved uncertainties, recommended next step and responsible decision-maker.

## 7. Data Quality
Do not invent missing shipment fields, supplier availability or root causes. If evidence is missing, conflicting or stale, state the limitation and request verification from the carrier or responsible team.
