# Shipment Delay Response Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure provides operational guidance for responding to a shipment that is delayed or identified as at risk.

## Immediate Response

When a shipment is confirmed as delayed:

1. Verify the shipment status and latest estimated arrival.
2. Confirm the origin, destination, carrier, and shipping mode.
3. Review available operational evidence for weather, route, carrier, or supplier-related contributors.
4. Escalate the shipment to the responsible operations team when the cause cannot be confirmed.
5. Do not change the shipment route, carrier, or supplier automatically.

## Investigation

Operations should distinguish between confirmed evidence and possible contributors.

A possible contributor must not be treated as a confirmed root cause unless supporting evidence is available.

Relevant evidence may include:

- Shipment status
- Estimated arrival
- Weather conditions
- Route conditions
- Carrier information
- Supplier information
- Company operating procedures

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The shipment is confirmed delayed.
- The estimated arrival has passed.
- Available evidence does not establish a reliable cause.
- An alternative route or supplier requires a business decision.

## Decision Authority

SentinelAI provides investigation findings and recommendations. Human operations staff remain responsible for decisions involving rerouting, carrier changes, supplier changes, customer commitments, or other operational actions.
