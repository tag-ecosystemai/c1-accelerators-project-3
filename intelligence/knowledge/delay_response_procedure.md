# Delay Response Procedure (SAMPLE)

## Early warning
If more than 48 hours remain before scheduled arrival, there is time to consider switching shipping mode. If fewer than 48 hours remain, rerouting is usually not possible, so focus on notifying the customer.

## Customer notification
Tell the customer about the likely delay as soon as the risk is confirmed.

## Escalation
High-value orders should be escalated to the operations manager before any change is made. The system recommends, and a human decides.
